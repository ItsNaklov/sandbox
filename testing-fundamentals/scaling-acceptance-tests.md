Acceptance tests are essential, and they directly impact your ability to confidently evole your system over time, with a reasonable cost of change.

They're also a great tool to help you work with legacy code. When faced with a poor codebase without any tests, resist the temptation to start refactoring. Instead, write some acceptance tests to give you a safety net to freely change the system's internals without affecting it's functional extarnal behaviours.
